Laws of Rwanda

One of the main responsibilities of the Rwanda Law Reform Commission (RLRC) is to ensure proper codification and coordination of Rwandan laws. It is in that framework that, since 2019, the Rwanda Law Reform Commission (RLRC) has published on its website the collection of Laws of Republic of Rwanda currently in force, referred to as “Laws of Rwanda”. This collection is updated every day by inserting new legislation or removing the repealed one.
